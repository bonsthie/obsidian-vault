# Implication

An implication:

$$
P \Rightarrow Q
$$

is **false only when**:

$$
P = \text{true}
\quad\text{and}\quad
Q = \text{false}
$$

| $P$ | $Q$ | $P \Rightarrow Q$ |
| --- | --- | ----------------- |
| T   | T   | T                 |
| T   | F   | **F**             |
| F   | T   | T                 |
| F   | F   | T                 |

> [!note]
> $P \Rightarrow Q$ does **not** mean that $P$ causes $Q$.
